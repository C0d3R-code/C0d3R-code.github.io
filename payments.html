PAYMENT QUESTIONS

A little clarity
before you pay.

Can I pay with a debit or credit card?

PayPal shows the methods available for your account, location, and checkout. This PayPal.Me link does not guarantee guest card checkout. If a suitable card option is unavailable, contact Xander to arrange the next step.

Should I pay the entire package price now?

Only pay the amount agreed with Xander for your project. The package selector is a reference; you can enter a different agreed amount for a partial payment.

Is the PHP calculator amount final?

No. It uses a reference exchange rate for estimates. Confirm the PHP amount with Xander before using GCash or Maya.

Will this page confirm my payment automatically?

No. Complete the payment in PayPal or your wallet, then send the transaction reference to Xander. Receipt is confirmed after checking the receiving account.
