A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we
get started.

How are website packages priced?

The total depends on your page count: one page is US$499, two pages are US$999, three pages are US$1,499, four pages are US$1,999, and five pages are US$2,499. Each page after three adds US$500. We’ll agree on the page count and project scope together.

Can I see the price in Philippine pesos?

Yes. The calculator converts the USD base price to an estimated PHP amount. It tries to load the latest daily reference rate and allows you to enter your own. The final conversion is confirmed before payment.

What should I prepare?

A short description of your project, the pages you need, your logo or brand references, and any text or images you already have are a useful starting point.

Can you add forms, payments, or an AI assistant?

Tell me what you need. External services, backend features, and integrations will be scoped with you before work begins.

How long will my website take?

The timeline depends on the page count, content, and features. Email me with your brief so we can confirm a realistic schedule.
