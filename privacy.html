Chat & privacy.

What happens when you use this website’s interactive features.

The AI portfolio assistant

The chat widget is provided by Botpress. Messages you enter are processed by Botpress to generate replies and may be available to the website owner in the Botpress workspace. Please avoid sharing passwords, payment information, or other sensitive details in chat.

The assistant answers questions about Xander’s portfolio and services. AI replies can be inaccurate. Sending a chat message does not send an email to Xander; contact him directly for proposals, feedback, or confirmed project terms.

Read the Botpress privacy policy ↗

The website estimate calculator

Your light or dark appearance preference is saved in your browser’s local storage when you use the theme toggle. Before you choose, the website follows your device’s color preference.

The calculator may request the latest daily USD exchange rate from ExchangeRate-API. A reference rate may be cached in your browser’s local storage. Calculations take place in your browser, and the PHP value is an estimate rather than a payment quote.

The inquiry form

The inquiry form prepares an email draft in your own email application. The form does not submit your brief to a server. Your email is sent only when you choose to send it in your email app.

Socials and payment services

Links to X open the social platform for project discussions. PayPal payment links open PayPal, where you review the recipient, amount, and available methods. GCash and Maya payments are completed in your wallet app using the supplied QR codes. Those services handle messages or payments under their own policies.

The website prepares payment links in your browser. It does not collect card or bank details, move funds between accounts, or automatically verify transfers.
